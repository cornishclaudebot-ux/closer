How it works

Three steps. One rule: be there.

Closer runs quietly in the background and only lights up when you're physically near another verified student.

1

Verify you're GCU

Sign in with your GCU email through Microsoft. Real students only. No catfish, no bots, no creeps from three states away.

2

Live your life

Go to class, the gym, the dining hall, or a party. Closer works in the background and notices who you genuinely cross paths with.

3

Cross paths to match

Come within about 20 feet of someone and you both unlock. Like them back and it's a match. You already shared a room. Now close the distance.

Proximity is the whole point

Other apps show you a thousand strangers you'll never meet. Closer only shows you people you were genuinely near, from your classes, your buildings, your scene. It's the difference between swiping and actually meeting.

Spotlight, if you want it

Opt in to the daily Spotlight board and see who's catching the most likes on campus this week. It's a game you choose to play, ranked by real likes, refreshed every day. Pure FOMO, fully your call.

Opt-in only

Match-gated by design

Nobody messages you until you both like each other. No surprise DMs, no spam. Closer shows you crossed paths after the fact, never your live location.

Your campus, not the internet

Everyone on Closer is a verified GCU student. The person you matched with sits two rows over, lives in your building, or was at the same event last night.

The unfair advantage

Closer lights up at every event.

Most dating apps are empty maps. Closer is built by a team that fills rooms with live events for a living. Every event becomes a live hotspot where matching turns on and the whole crowd is right there with you.

Questions

The stuff you're wondering.

Who can join Closer?

Verified GCU students who are 18 and over. You sign in with your GCU email through Microsoft, so everyone on Closer is a real, current student.

How do you know people are real?

Two checks. Your GCU email confirms you're a student, and an AI photo match ties your face to your Instagram for the verified check. Real faces, no bots, no catfish.

Does Closer track my location?

Closer uses proximity to notice when you cross paths with someone, but your live location is never shown to anyone, ever. You only see that you crossed paths, after the fact, never where a person is right now.

How is this different from Tinder or Hinge?

You only match with people you were physically near, on your own campus. No endless feed of strangers, no bots, no one three states away. The people you see are people you'd actually run into.

What does it cost?

Joining and matching are free. Closer+ is an optional upgrade for extras like unlimited priority matches and your full crossed-paths history. You never have to pay to meet people.

When does it launch?

Closer opens at GCU first. Join the waitlist and we'll text you the moment you're in. Founding members get in first and keep founding perks.

Is it safe?

Safety is built in. No live location, verified students only, messaging only after you both match, one-tap block and report, opt-in Spotlight, and a one-strike ban policy for anyone who breaks the rules.

What if the people I want aren't on it yet?

Invite them. Closer gets better the more of your circle joins, and inviting friends earns you perks. Your circle is smaller than you think, and bigger than you can imagine.
